The moment `tbiom/reports/EXP_FREQBLENDER.md` lands, the recipe is fixed and FaceShifter is scored **once** with the
final system and reported. Its source-split audit (2026-09-17, metadata only) established that its 140 ids are exactly

## 2. Experiment 7 — Step 1, then PPO if the controls justify it

The Step-1 controls run first and the go/no-go below is fixed before they are scored. PPO runs only if all three
conditions hold, and is compared against an equal-budget multi-step greedy policy.

**Go/no-go, frozen 2026-09-18, on the selection folds of the same source-grouped splits:**
1. **Headroom.** An oracle allowed 1–2 additional window inspections improves video AUROC by **≥ 0.005** over the
   no-acquisition reference. Without headroom there is nothing for a policy to find.
2. **Learnability.** The supervised one-step action-value selector beats the better of fixed-uniform and random
   selection by **≥ 0.002** mean AUROC at equal budget.
3. **Variation.** Which action helps actually varies by video: the oracle's chosen window is the modal window on
   **< 60%** of videos. If one window is almost always right, the answer is a fixed rule, not a policy.

A bounded two-step assessment is reported beside the one-step numbers, so a lack of one-step benefit does not by
itself rule out sequential value. Cost is counted for decoding, quality estimation and the controller, not only for
detector calls. Reward if PPO runs: `r_t = loss(p_t, y) − loss(p_{t+1}, y) − λ·cost(a_t)`; policies are selected on a
predefined development objective and reported with AUROC **and** log loss, FPR/TPR and cost. Full-population results
are reported separately from any abstention result.
