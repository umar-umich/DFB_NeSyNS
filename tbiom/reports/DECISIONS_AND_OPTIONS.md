## 3. Open decisions — recorded for discussion, not taken

### Stage 3 route 2 — TODO(run)

### Override A — adopt arm E for processing robustness (route 3)

Arm E does not meet the criteria against the deployed views (§2, last row). Adopting it anyway trades clean and reenactment for processed robustness; three training seeds are measured so the trade is quantified rather than assumed. Reported as an override wherever used.

### Override B — adopt FreqBlender for its in-the-wild gain

FreqBlender improves FFIW by +0.0431 on development and +0.0385 on test under DS-R, and FFIW's own false positives *fall* (0.054 → 0.029). It failed only on pooled false positives (+0.024 against a +0.02 limit — a near miss). DeepfakeEval2024 gives no support either way (−0.0035 development, +0.0031 test). Adopting it would override the frozen limit and must be labelled as such (`EXP_FREQBLENDER.md`).

### Override C — report T + Off-manifold as the headline system

Appearance earns little beside the other two (+0.0012 under DS-R, −0.0050 under logistic, CI excluding zero in 0/5 splits). The decision of 2026-09-18 keeps three views and reports the ablation honestly; revisiting it would simplify the system at the cost of the three-view claim (`EXP_THREEVIEW_ABLATION.md`).

## 4. How to read the rejections

Each came with the control that makes it interpretable: a null oracle that out-performed the real one (acquisition), quality descriptors that help in-sample and hurt across source groups (reliability), dense windows that are order-sensitive where our stored stills are not (temporal), and a crop-geometry confound caught and withdrawn before it could become a result (FreqBlender v1). Those controls are the reportable content of a negative.
